Snake — How to play

It's the classic Snake game (try it at playsnake.org), turned into a two-player, turn-based match: each player controls one snake and they alternate turns. Grow by eating apples and don't crash.

Board

  • A grid rendered as a string (rows wrapped in |...|).
  • Your snake: A/B for the head, lowercase for the body.
  • Food: *   Empty: space.
  • From 9 Sep 2026 the food is numbers, not *: five single digits (1–9) are on the board at once and you must eat them in order (see Rules). Empty is still a space. From 30 Sep 2026 each digit appears in several copies (3–5).
  • From 16 Sep 2026 the board also holds X pickups: eating one is worth +50 and stacks your score multiplier (x2, then x3, …) for the rest of the match (see Rules).
  • From 23 Sep 2026 the board also holds a # wall: a straight line (horizontal or vertical) that shrinks toward its centre each round. Running into a # costs -500 and your snake stays put (see Rules).
  • From 7 Oct 2026 the board can hold tail food, Ⓐ / Ⓑ (the letter in a circle is who may eat it): when a snake crashes, the end of its body turns into tail food that only the opponent can eat (see Rules).
  • From 14 Oct 2026 the board also holds proximity bombs: two big 💣 and five small 🧨. They go off when your snake's head gets near them — within 2 cells of a 💣, within 1 cell of a 🧨 — and wipe your score to 0. They disappear after round 150 (see Rules).
  • From 21 Oct 2026 * is back, as shot food: when a shot hits a snake, the cut-off part of its body turns into * that either snake can eat (see Shooting below).
  • From 28 Oct 2026 the board can hold a star, ⭐ (one at a time from round 20, and lots of them in the last 100 moves): eating it counts as food and makes your snake invincible for 50 turns. An invincible snake is drawn inverted: lowercase head (a/b), uppercase body (A/B) (see Star below).
  • From 4 Nov 2026 the board is a 3D cube of 20×20×20 (levels × rows × cols). You no longer get the whole board: only the three planes through your snake’s head, board_x, board_y and board_z (see 3D cube below).
  • The board size varies from match to match (from 2 Sep 2026): each dimension is chosen at random between 12 and 20 (between 20 and 30 from 14 Oct 2026, to make room around the bombs), so the board is not necessarily square. Read rows, cols and board_size from turn_data each game — don't assume 15×15. From 4 Nov 2026 it is a fixed 20×20×20 cube.

Your turn

Send a direction and your snake advances one cell:

{ "action": "move", "data": { "direction": "up" } }

direction is one of up, down, left, right.

From 4 Nov 2026 you can also go up or down a level in the cube, with ascend (level − 1) and descend (level + 1). up/down/left/right still move within your level:

{ "action": "move", "data": { "direction": "ascend" } }

From 21 Oct 2026 you can shoot instead of moving (it costs 5000 points, see Shooting below):

{ "action": "shoot", "data": { "direction": "right" } }

Rules & scoring

  • Eat food (*) to grow: +100, and new food spawns.
  • Surviving a move: +1.
  • Crashing into a wall, your own body, or the opponent ends the game: you lose -500, the opponent wins +1000. From 7 Oct 2026 a crash no longer ends the game (see Crashes below).
  • If nobody crashes within the remaining moves, the higher score wins.

Numbered food (from 9 Sep 2026): instead of *, the board shows five digits you must eat in ascending, cyclic order (…7, 8, 9, 1, 2… — there is no 0). At the start the digits 1–5 are on the board; each time the current one is eaten, the next digit in the sequence appears, so five consecutive digits are always in play.

  • Eating the correct next digit grows you and scores digit × 100 (so a 1 scores 100, a 9 scores 900).
  • Eating any other digit is a penalty of -500 and that same digit reappears elsewhere.
  • The next digit to eat is the one on the board whose cyclic predecessor (…8, 9, 1…) is not present — compute it from the board.

Careful — the next digit is not simply the smallest one on the board. Because the five-digit window slides and wraps past 9, once it holds both high and low digits the target is the one continuing the sequence, not the lowest. For example, with 1 6 7 8 9 on the board the next digit is 6 (you already ate 1–5), not 1 — eating that 1 is a −500 penalty. Always derive the target from the cyclic-predecessor rule above, don't just grab the smallest number.

Multipliers (from 16 Sep 2026): two X pickups sit on the board alongside the food. Eating an X scores a flat +50 and bumps your multiplier one step — x2 after the first, x3 after the second, and so on — and a replacement X appears elsewhere.

  • The multiplier applies to food points only: eating the correct digit scores digit × 100 × multiplier (a 9 at x3 is 2700).
  • The +50 of the X itself, the +1 per move and the penalties (-500) are not multiplied.
  • It is permanent: once earned it lasts the whole match and keeps stacking. Eating a wrong digit does not reset it.
  • An X does not grow your snake, and each multiplier is per player — read yours from multiplier_1 / multiplier_2 in turn_data.

Walls (from 23 Sep 2026): the board holds one # wall at a time — a straight line (horizontal or vertical) of an odd length up to 11, placed at random on empty cells.

  • Running your head into a # is a -500 penalty and your snake does not move that turn — the game continues (it is not an instant game over).
  • After both players have moved, the wall shrinks toward its centre (it loses one cell at each end), so a length-11 wall goes 11→9→7→ …→1→gone.
  • Once a wall is gone a new one appears elsewhere. Read it straight from the board (the # cells); there is no separate field.

Multiple copies (from 30 Sep 2026): each of the five window digits now appears in several copies (a random 3–5 per digit) instead of a single cell, so the next digit can't be monopolised by camping one spot.

  • The order rule is unchanged: eat the target digit (the cyclic-predecessor one). It scores digit × 100 × multiplier once and grows you once, for the single copy you ate.
  • Eating the target removes every remaining copy of that digit and the next digit appears in its own 3–5 copies. So only one copy of each digit is ever worth points.
  • Eating a wrong digit is still a -500 penalty (only that copy is consumed and a replacement of the same digit reappears).

Crashes and longer matches (from 7 Oct 2026): matches last 400 moves (200 per player) instead of 300, and crashing — into your own body, the board edge, a # wall or the opponent — no longer ends the game. Instead:

  • Your snake does not move that turn (it stays where it was, like with a #).
  • It loses its tail: it keeps its first 3 cells (head + 2) and the rest of its body turns into tail food for the opponent. A snake of 3 cells loses nothing.
  • Your first crash wipes your score to 0. Every later crash is the usual -500. If your score is already 0 or negative at your first crash, the usual -500 applies — crashing is never a way to clear a negative score (it still counts as your first crash).
  • The opponent gets nothing for your crash (no +1000). As before, falling below -2500 ends the game and you lose.
  • Tail food is drawn with the letter of the snake that may eat it, in a circle: Ⓐ (U+24B6) can only be eaten by A, Ⓑ (U+24B7) only by B. So your crash feeds your rival, never you. Eating it, in any order and at any time, is +100 × your multiplier and you grow one cell. It is not part of the digit sequence (no penalty) and is not replaced once eaten. In the JSON these arrive as "Ⓐ" / "Ⓑ" — one character each once decoded.
  • The snake that shed it can step on it: the cell is cleared, with no points and no growth (a normal +1 move). That is a way to deny the food to your rival.
  • From 14 Oct 2026, tail cells that would land inside a bomb’s blast zone just vanish (nothing edible is ever within reach of a bomb).

Proximity bombs (from 14 Oct 2026): two big 💣 and five small 🧨 bombs sit on the board, and boards grow to 20–30 per side to leave room around them. You don't have to touch them: a bomb explodes as soon as your snake's head moves within its reach.

  • 💣 (big) explodes when your head ends a move 2 cells or closer to it; 🧨 (small) at 1 cell or closer. Diagonals count as one cell (the distance is max(|Δrow|, |Δcol|)), so the blast zone is a 5×5 square around a 💣 and a 3×3 square around a 🧨.
  • An explosion wipes your score to 0 (if your score is already 0 or negative, it is a -500 penalty instead). Two bombs in reach at once count once. Your move still happens — you eat whatever was on the cell — you keep your tail, and the game continues. An explosion is not a crash.
  • Nothing edible is ever inside a blast zone: food and X never appear within reach of a bomb, and bombs never appear with food or an X within their reach. Everything you need to eat can be eaten without setting a bomb off.
  • The exploded bomb disappears and a new one of the same size appears elsewhere, never within reach of either snake's head.
  • Only the head triggers bombs (your body only goes where your head has already been).
  • After round 150 (once each player has made 150 moves, i.e. with 100 moves left) every bomb disappears for good: the end of the match is bomb-free.

Shooting (from 21 Oct 2026): on your turn you can send "action": "shoot" with a direction instead of moving. Your snake does not move that turn; the shot flies from your head in that direction.

  • Every shot costs 5000 points, hit or miss. You need a score of at least 5000 to shoot: with less, the shot doesn't fire and it is an invalid action (the usual -500).
  • The shot is instant and goes in a straight line. It passes over your own body, food, X and bombs (it doesn't set them off), and stops at the board edge, a # wall (walls are cover) or the first cell of the opponent's snake.
  • A hit cuts the opponent from the hit cell to the end of its tail. Hitting the head leaves just the head (a snake of length 1). A shot can only hit the opponent, never your own snake.
  • The cut cells turn into * shot food that either snake can eat, in any order: +100 × your multiplier and you grow one cell. It is not part of the digit sequence (no penalty) and is not replaced once eaten. Cut cells inside a bomb’s blast zone just vanish.
  • The opponent loses no points: being shot is not a crash (no score wipe, no -500, it doesn't count as its first crash). It just loses length — and maybe the race to eat its own tail back.
  • The shot is echoed back in play_data as "action": "shoot" plus its direction.

Star (from 28 Oct 2026): now and then a ⭐ appears on the board. Eat it (move onto it) and your snake is invincible for your next 50 turns.

  • Eating the star is like eating a *: +100 × your multiplier and you grow one cell (it is not part of the digit sequence, no penalty). Eating another star while invincible restarts the count at 50. turn_data counts the turns left in invincible_1 / invincible_2 (0 = not invincible). Every turn of yours counts, whatever you do (move, shoot, invalid action or timeout).
  • While invincible your snake is drawn inverted: the head is lowercase (a/b) and the body uppercase (A/B). Find your head by its case and invincible_N, not by the case alone.
  • You lose no points for anything. Running into the board edge, a # wall or your own body: you just stay put (no -500, no score wipe, no tail lost, it doesn't count as your first crash). Bombs still go off when you get near, but don't touch your score. Eating a digit out of order costs nothing (it still reappears elsewhere and you grow). Invalid actions cost nothing either (but 10 timeouts still lose the match). Shots still cost 5000: that's spending, not losing.
  • Running into the rival cuts it like a shot, for free: from the cell you hit to the end of its tail (hitting the head leaves just the head), and those cells become * shot food anyone can eat. You stay put. The rival loses no points. This works even if the rival is invincible too.
  • Shots can't cut an invincible snake: a shot that hits it stops there and cuts nothing (the shooter still pays 5000). A rival that isn't invincible running into it crashes as usual.
  • When it appears: there is never more than one star. The first one appears after round 20 (once each player has made 20 moves). While a snake is invincible there is no star; when its 50 turns run out, the next star appears 20 rounds later.
  • Where: on a free cell at the same distance from both heads (counting |Δrow| + |Δcol| and ignoring obstacles), as close as the board allows, and never inside a bomb's blast zone. It's a race.
  • Star frenzy: when remaining_moves reaches 100 (round 150 of a 400-move match, when the bombs go away) the board fills up with 10 stars on random free cells. Every star eaten is replaced right away, until the end of the match. Both snakes can be invincible at once, and invincible snakes cut each other.

3D cube (from 4 Nov 2026): the board is a 20×20×20 cube. A cell is (level, row, col); level 0 is the top one, like row 0 is the top row. Every rule above still applies, in three dimensions.

  • Moving: ascend goes one level up (level − 1), descend one level down (level + 1). up/down/left/right move within your level as before. Both snakes start on the middle level (level 10), in the same spots as on a flat board.
  • What you see: only the three planes that go through your head, each drawn like a flat board (|...| lines):
    • board_z: your level seen from above, rows × cols (the classic board).
    • board_y: your row seen from the front, levels × cols (line i is level i).
    • board_x: your column seen from the side, levels × rows (line i is level i, character j is row j).
    head tells you where your head is ({"level": 10, "row": 1, "col": 2}), and board holds the three planes in the order x, y, z, separated by a blank line.
  • Anything off your planes is invisible: food, pickups, walls, bombs, stars and the rival. You see the rival only when it crosses one of your planes. Remember what you have seen: the cube doesn’t change much from one turn to the next.
  • Edges: the cube has six faces. Leaving through the top (ascend from level 0) or the bottom (descend from level 19) is a crash like any other edge.
  • More of everything, so the cube isn’t empty: each window digit comes in 10–15 copies (instead of 3–5), there are 6 X (instead of 2), 6 💣 and 15 🧨 (instead of 2 and 5), and the star frenzy brings 30 stars (instead of 10). Food order and points are unchanged.
  • Walls are square plates: a # wall is now a square of odd side (3–11) lying flat on one axis: a floor/ceiling or a vertical wall. Each round it loses its outer ring (11×11 → 9×9 → … → 1 → gone). On your planes a plate shows up as a line or as a square.
  • Bombs reach in 3D: the distance is max(|Δlevel|, |Δrow|, |Δcol|), so the blast zone is a 5×5×5 cube around a 💣 and 3×3×3 around a 🧨. A bomb on the level above or below can go off even if it is not on any of your planes. Nothing edible is ever inside a blast zone, and bombs still disappear after round 150.
  • Shooting works in all six directions, including ascend/descend.
  • The star appears at the same distance from both heads, now counting |Δlevel| + |Δrow| + |Δcol|, and on a cell that both snakes can see when it appears (on one of each head’s planes).

turn_data

Each turn you receive board, rows, cols, remaining_moves, your side, and player_1/2 / score_1/2.

From 2 Sep 2026 you also receive board_size, the board dimensions as a "rows x cols" string (e.g. "15x18").

From 16 Sep 2026 you also receive multiplier_1 and multiplier_2, each player's current score multiplier (starts at 1). It is the only piece of state you cannot read off the board.

From 28 Oct 2026 you also receive invincible_1 and invincible_2: how many turns of star invincibility each player has left (0 = not invincible).

From 4 Nov 2026 you also receive levels (20), head ({"level", "row", "col"} of your head) and the three planes board_x, board_y, board_z; board holds those same three planes and board_size is "levels x rows x cols" ("20x20x20"). There is no field with the whole cube.

Read the food and snake positions from the board: food is * (or a digit 1–9 from 9 Sep 2026; from 7 Oct 2026 Ⓐ/Ⓑ are tail food only A/B can eat; from 21 Oct 2026 * is shot food anyone can eat), X is a multiplier pickup (from 16 Sep 2026), # is a wall (from 23 Sep 2026), 💣/🧨 are big/small proximity bombs (from 14 Oct 2026), ⭐ is the star (from 28 Oct 2026), A/B are the snake heads and a/b their bodies (inverted while that snake is invincible, from 28 Oct 2026). They are not provided as separate fields.

Non-ASCII symbols — read the board by character, not by byte. From 7 Oct 2026 the board can contain Ⓐ/Ⓑ (tail food), and from 14 Oct 2026 💣/🧨 (bombs). Each cell is still exactly one Unicode character (code point), so split rows into characters, not bytes or UTF-16 units:

  • Python: list(row) / row[i] just work.
  • JavaScript / TypeScript: Array.from(row) or [...row].
  • Java: row.codePoints().toArray(); C#: row.EnumerateRunes().
  • Go: []rune(row); Rust: row.chars().

Ⓐ/Ⓑ in JavaScript: they are U+24B6 and U+24B7, inside the Basic Multilingual Plane, so each one is a single UTF-16 unit: row.length and row[i] keep working (the same holds in Java and C#). In the JSON they arrive as "\u24b6" / "\u24b7" and JSON.parse turns them back into one character. Compare against the escapes, so your source file's encoding doesn't matter:

const TAIL_FOOD = { A: '\u24B6', B: '\u24B7' };   // Ⓐ / Ⓑ
const cells = [...row];             // safe for every symbol on the board
if (cells[c] === TAIL_FOOD[side]) {
  // tail food only *you* can eat (+100 x multiplier)
} else if (Object.values(TAIL_FOOD).includes(cells[c])) {
  // your own shed tail: stepping on it just clears it
}

Only code that indexes raw bytes (Go row[i] on a string, C char*) sees them as several units: each is 3 bytes in UTF-8.

This matters for the bombs: 💣 and 🧨 are outside the Basic Multilingual Plane, so in JavaScript, Java and C# row.length and row[i] count each of them as two units and every later column shifts. In the JSON they arrive as surrogate-pair escapes ("\ud83d\udca3", "\ud83e\udde8"), which any JSON decoder turns back into one character.

The star ⭐ (from 28 Oct 2026) is U+2B50, inside the Basic Multilingual Plane like Ⓐ/Ⓑ: one UTF-16 unit, "⭐" in the JSON. It is sent bare, without the U+FE0F emoji variation selector, so it is always exactly one character.

Cambios en las reglas

Las reglas evolucionan con el tiempo. Cada cambio se implementa como una versión numerada del juego con una fecha de activación; mantén tu bot preparado para las próximas novedades.

  • v11 — 4 de noviembre de 2026: el tablero es un cubo de 20×20×20. Nuevas direcciones ascend/ descend. Cada robot ve solo los tres planos a través de su cabeza ( board_x, board_y, board_z, más heady levels). 10–15 copias por dígito, 6 X, 6 💣+ 15 🧨con zonas de explosión cúbicas, 30 estrellas de frenesí. Las paredes son placas cuadradas que se encogen; la estrella aparece donde ambas cabezas pueden verla.
  • v10 — 28 oct. 2026: una ⭐estrella (una a la vez desde la ronda 20, la siguiente 20 rondas después de que termine una invencibilidad, equidistante de ambas cabezas; 10 a la vez, reemplazadas a medida que se comen, en los últimos 100 movimientos). Comerla es como comer un *(+100 × multiplicador, creces) y hace que tu serpiente sea invencible durante 50 turnos ( invincible_1/2en turn_data, dibujada con una cabeza minúscula y un cuerpo mayúscula). No pierde puntos por nada, los choques la dejan en su lugar, y chocar con el rival la corta como un disparo (incluso un rival invencible). Los disparos no la cortan.
  • v9 — 21 oct. 2026: Disparo: envía "action": "shoot"con un directionen lugar de moverte. Cuesta 5000 (acierto o fallo; necesitas al menos 5000), vuela en línea recta, las #paredes lo detienen y corta al oponente desde la celda impactada hasta su cola. Las celdas cortadas se convierten en *comida que cualquiera puede comer (+100 × multiplicador). El oponente no pierde puntos.
  • v8 — 14 oct. 2026: Bombas de proximidad: dos grandes 💣explotan cuando una cabeza de serpiente se acerca a 2 casillas, cinco pequeñas 🧨a 1 casilla; una explosión borra la puntuación 0 y la bomba reaparece en otro lugar. Nunca hay nada comestible dentro de una zona de explosión. Después de la ronda 150, todas las bombas desaparecen. Los tableros crecen a 20-30 por lado.
  • v7 — 7 oct. 2026: los choques (cuerpo propio, borde del tablero, #pared u oponente) ya no terminan la partida: la serpiente se queda donde está, conserva 3 casillas y el resto de su cuerpo se convierte en comida para la cola que solo el oponente puede comer ( Ⓐ/ Ⓑ, +100, cualquier orden). El primer choque borra la puntuación a 0; los siguientes son -500 . Las partidas duran 400 movimientos en lugar de 300.
  • v6 — 30 Sep 2026: cada dígito ordenado aparece en varias copias (3–5) en lugar de una; al comer el dígito objetivo se eliminan todas sus copias y se avanza, por lo que la secuencia no puede ser monopolizada.
  • v5 — 23 de septiembre de 2026: aparece una #pared en el tablero (una línea recta de hasta 11 casillas de largo) que se estrecha hacia su centro en cada ronda; chocar contra ella supone una penalización de -500 y tu serpiente se queda en su sitio.
  • v4 — 16 de septiembre de 2026: X aparecen recogidas en el tablero: comer una otorga +50 puntos y acumula un multiplicador de puntuación permanente ( x2, x3, …) que multiplica tus puntos de comida; turn_dataobtiene multiplier_1/2.
  • v3 — 9 Sep 2026: la comida se convierte en números en lugar de *: come cinco dígitos a bordo ( 1– 9) en orden cíclico ascendente; el dígito correcto obtiene dígito × 100 , uno incorrecto es una penalización de -500 .
  • v2 — 2 de septiembre de 2026: el tamaño del tablero varía según la partida (cada dimensión es aleatoria entre 12 y 20, no necesariamente cuadrada) y your_turnse añade un board_sizecampo ( "rows x cols").
  • v1: tablero fijo de 15×15.
